# Bad Monster, Good Robot
A little game. 

Final assignment for the Advanced Course in Programming 
at the University of Helsinki, Open University.
Assignment completed in May of 2026.

Author: Juan Ignacio Mendoza Garay

Instructions:
  * Get rich or get eaten by monsters (<img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/monster.png?raw=true" width="10" title="monster" alt="monster"/> <img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/monster.png?raw=true" width="10" title="monster" alt="monster"/>). 
  * A robot (<img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/robot.png?raw=true" width="10" title="robot" alt="robot"/>) can take your coins (<img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/coin.png?raw=true" width="10" title="coin" alt="coin"/> <img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/coin.png?raw=true" width="10" title="coin" alt="coin"/> <img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/coin.png?raw=true" width="10" title="coin" alt="coin"/>) and save them for you.
  * Through a door (<img src="https://github.com/juigmend/Bad_Monster_Good_Robot/blob/main/door.png?raw=true" width="10" title="door" alt="door"/>) you can escape to a clear space.
  * You are at the center of the screen and see the action from above.
  * Move with the arrow keys.
  * Change the game parameters to adjust difficulty and visualisation.
